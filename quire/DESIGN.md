# Quire product page

Quire makes books, so the page reads like the bench where one is made: warm paper as the ground, ink for text, the app's own brass (`#8a6420`) for the one action that matters (the download) and for links. Headings use Palatino Linotype, the default face Quire typesets in, from the reader's own system; nothing is downloaded for it.

Every image is evidence: either the real window or a page Quire really typeset, each listed in `captures/PROVENANCE.md`. Window captures sit on the dark bench colour; typeset pages sit on the paper itself, as pages would.

The credits band states what Quire does and, separately, what it does not claim: that tests verify paper, that the download is publisher-signed, or that the source is open.
