AFK AI

Local AI for Windows, without the plumbing becoming the product.

AFK AI checks the PC, sets up the runtime and a model, and starts chat on this machine. When something is missing it names the one next step, and it refuses to touch containers it cannot prove it owns.

DemiMedia

A Windows film player that lets the picture lead and keeps the detail one step away.

You choose the result you want, such as faithful picture or smoother motion. DemiMedia shows what you requested, what it planned and what the player actually reported, and shows when playback had to change path.

ValClips

Turns long gameplay sessions into ranked, evidence-grounded stories worth making.

It reads a whole sitting, ranks the moments that make a story, previews the edit, and renders only after you choose how it should feel. Every claim in a clip has to be backed by what was on screen.

The working standard

  1. Observe first. Measure the machine, the file or the recording before acting on it.
  2. Tell the truth about state. Requested, planned and observed are different things, and unknown stays unknown.
  3. Own the boring parts. Setup, recovery, diagnostics and failure handling are part of the product.
  4. Keep people out of the machinery. Technical depth should lower the burden, not become a badge.
