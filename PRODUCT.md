# Product

## Register

Brand

## Purpose

Allusions is the maker and umbrella identity for focused software spanning local AI, difficult media, and gameplay production. This site gives visitors a concise project register, makes each product's maturity legible, and explains the working standard shared across the studio.

## Audience

People encountering one Allusions product who want to understand the maker behind it, and technically curious visitors evaluating the studio's broader body of work. A first-time visitor should understand what Allusions is, what its three projects do, how mature each one is, and where to go next within twenty seconds.

## Product truth

- AFK AI is in Beta and its product site is the authority for downloads and exact release information.
- DemiMedia is in development and its public repository is the authority for current engineering status.
- ValClips is in private development and has no public product surface, repository link, or download.
- The studio site is not itself a product release channel.

## Constraints

- Static HTML and CSS; no JavaScript required.
- Self-hosted assets only; no analytics, trackers, remote fonts, or CDN dependencies.
- Strict default-deny Content Security Policy.
- Product destinations remain authoritative and must be checked before public deployment.
- Do not introduce retired release terminology or imply unshipped availability.
