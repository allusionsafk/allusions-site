Nothing is published before you check it.

The story, its title, its proof and where it goes are reviewed together. A finished render is not an approval: until you have reviewed it, ValClips will not publish, and the checks run again on the exact file right before any upload.

What it is, and what it is not

It is

  • An editor that works on the recordings your capture tool already makes. It does not record.
  • Local: sessions are read, ranked and rendered on your PC.
  • Strict about proof: a kill counts only when the evidence ties it to your accounts.

It is not

  • A replacement for your recorder or clipping tool.
  • A promise of views. It finds stories worth making; it does not predict how they will do.
  • Available yet. There is no public download or public repository.
